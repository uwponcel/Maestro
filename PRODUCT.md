# Maestro Product

## Register

product

## Users

Guild Wars 2 players using Blish HUD while actively playing instruments, browsing
songs, composing arrangements, or practicing performances. The interface must stay
compact, readable over the game, and quick to operate without pulling attention away
from gameplay.

## Product Purpose

Maestro makes playing music in Guild Wars 2 approachable and expressive. It combines
reliable song playback with practice tools, creation, importing, playlists, and
community sharing in one local-first Blish HUD module.

## Brand Personality

Warm, musical, crafted, and appreciative. Maestro should feel like a polished fan-made
instrument rather than a generic utility or commercial storefront.

## Anti-references

- Promotional overlays that interrupt playback or pressure players to contribute.
- Generic web-dashboard layouts that ignore Guild Wars 2 and Blish HUD conventions.
- Overdecorated controls, loud donation banners, or novelty interactions that obscure
  the primary music workflow.
- Dense instructions when a concise label, tooltip, or familiar action is enough.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the game primary: every surface should be compact, dismissible, and legible
   over a busy game scene.
2. Preserve familiar Blish HUD interactions so controls feel native to the overlay.
3. Use Maestro's warm musical identity for hierarchy and delight, not decoration that
   competes with the task.
4. Make important actions obvious and reversible, with clear feedback after clicks.
5. Treat community participation and support as optional, grateful invitations rather
   than requirements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Do not communicate state through color alone. Pair icons with labels or tooltips,
maintain readable contrast against translucent game backgrounds, keep click targets
consistent with existing Blish HUD controls, and avoid motion that delays interaction.
